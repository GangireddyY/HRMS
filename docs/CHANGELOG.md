## [1.11.0] – 2026-11-07 – Assistant and insight dashboards

### Added
- **HRMS Assistant**: a side panel (top bar → Ask AI, Ctrl/⌘+J, or "Ask AI" at the bottom of search results) that answers questions from live HR data, limited to what the user may see: pending approvals, leave balances, own requests, who is out, holidays, latest payslip, today's attendance, headcount, open jobs, policies to acknowledge, people lookup; otherwise it suggests matching pages. Questions are matched by rules in the browser (`modules/assistant/engine.ts`); no data leaves the application.
- Reusable components: `AppHero` (greeting with live summary and actions), `AppInsights` (computed observations with a link to act), `AppActionQueue` (approvals waiting for you), `AppPersonList`, `AppQuickActions`, `AppSection`, `AppSparkline` (also on `AppStatCard` through `spark`). Approvals are gathered by `modules/insights/approvals.ts`, by permission and plan.

### Changed
- HR, employee, administration and platform dashboards are rebuilt around these: a hero summary, KPI trends, a "waiting for you" queue, insights, who's out this week and what's coming up.
- Sidebar sections collapse (remembered per browser); pages have a soft gradient wash at the top.
- No more narrow centred pages: payslip, announcement, survey, suggestions, resignation and exit letter pages use the full width with a side panel (`AppSplit`, `AppFacts`) — net pay and attendance next to the payslip, progress and the send button next to a survey, "what happens next" next to the resignation form. The Company feed shows upcoming events and recognition beside it; the job pipeline's columns stretch to fill the screen; short card grids end with an "add" tile (`AppAddTile`).
- **Phone and tablet layout** (below 1024px, decided in one place by `useDevice()`): instead of a shrunken desktop with a drawer, an app layout — a frosted top bar (Back on inner pages, workspace or page title, search, notifications, profile), a floating tab bar with three key pages per workspace (`mobileTabs` in `workspaces.ts`, filtered by permission), Ask AI in the middle and **More** (profile, workspaces, every page of the workspace, organizations, dark mode, sign out). The tab bar steps aside while a form is open; form actions become full-width buttons above the home indicator; notifications appear at the top; page actions scroll in one row; safe areas of notched phones are respected (`viewport-fit=cover`, home-screen app meta tags).
- Form pages have a side panel on wide screens (`AppFormGuide`): required fields still empty (click to jump to one), errors to fix, shortcuts (Ctrl/⌘+Enter saves, Esc goes back) and Ask AI. Forms add their own context through AppModal's `aside` slot — Apply for leave shows your balances there, and picking one selects that leave type.

## [1.10.1] – 2026-11-07 – Profile photos

### Added
- Profile photos (migration `20261107110000_user_avatars`: `user_avatars`, `users.avatar_url`). Everyone uploads their own on My profile (`POST /auth/me/avatar`, PNG, JPG or WebP up to 5 MB; the type is read from the file, SVG refused), removes it (`DELETE /auth/me/avatar`). The image is cropped to a 256 × 256 square around the most interesting area and re-encoded as WebP, which strips metadata such as GPS. `GET /users/:id/avatar?v=…` serves it only with the version from the photo URL, so photos can't be fetched by user id alone. Shown in the top bar, account home, users list and detail, employees list and profile; avatars fall back to initials. Test: `avatars.e2e-spec.ts`.

## [1.10.0] – 2026-11-06 – Lifecycle completion

Builds the deliverables that were still open in the phase plan (PRD §4, §8).

### Added
- **Exit letters and alumni** (migration `20261107040000_exit_letters_alumni`: `exit_letters`, `alumni_profiles`). HR (`exit.manage`) drafts and issues relieving and experience letters once an exit is completed (never for an exit on death), numbered per type (`REL-0001`, `EXP-0001`), with the text fixed at issue, and revokes them with a reason; the employee sees and prints issued letters from My Workspace. Former employees opt in to the alumni directory with the contact details they choose; HR and managers with team-wide exit access browse it (HR operations → Alumni).
- **Loans and salary advances** (migration `20261107050000_employee_loans`; permissions `payroll.loan.apply` — employee self-service — `payroll.loan.approve` and `payroll.loan.manage`). Requests by employees or HR; approval by someone other than the requester fixes amount, tenure, interest on the reducing balance (loans) and the first deduction month; pay-out with a bank reference; payroll runs deduct the instalment (`LOAN_…` lines) and record repayments on approval; deferral, manual repayments and automatic closing; the full & final settlement recovers what's still owed. Rules in `loan-rules.ts` / `loan-balances.ts` (unit-tested). Web: My Workspace → Loans & advances; HR operations → Payroll → Loans.
- **Attendance** (migration `20261107060000_attendance_extended`; permission `attendance.wfh.apply` for employees):
  - Overtime: shifts set whether overtime counts, after how many minutes and the minimum; overtime is recorded per day (all minutes on a day off), approved or rejected by the manager in scope, blocks closing the period while pending, and approved minutes are paid by payroll (`OVERTIME` earning at the organization's multiplier on basic or gross).
  - Work from home: requests for up to 31 days, approved by the manager in scope; approved days are attendance in WFH mode. Locations can have a geofence (latitude, longitude, radius); web punch-ins from outside it are refused except on approved work-from-home days.
  - Rosters: rotation patterns and a day-by-day roster (assign, apply a rotation, clear; up to 92 days) that overrides the default shift for attendance, rest days and leave day counts.
  - Attendance terminals: registered devices with a secret key (shown once, rotatable, stored hashed) push batches of up to 500 punches identified by the employee's biometric code to `POST /attendance/device/punches`; repeats are ignored.
  - Web: Approvals (regularizations, WFH, overtime), Roster, Devices, overtime fields on shifts, geofences on locations, the WFH request from My attendance.
- **Benefit renewals** (migration `20261107070000_benefit_renewals`): renew a plan into a draft for the next policy period (options copied); activating it carries active enrolments over (same option or the default, with the dependants still covered) for employees still eligible; payroll deducts only the latest plan of a renewal chain.
- **Skill matrix** (migration `20261107080000_skills`): skill catalog, required skills and levels per designation, skills awarded by courses on completion, self-assessed skills with manager / HR verification (never one's own), a matrix of people × skills with gaps. Web: My Workspace → My skills; HR operations → Training → Skills.
- **Salary-hike recommendations** (migration `20261107090000_hike_recommendations`): per cycle, merit guidelines (minimum, target and maximum increase per final rating), a budget (% of payroll) and the date increases take effect (`performance.configure`). The reviewer of a completed review recommends an increase and an optional promotion — a justification is required outside the guideline — and submits it; someone with `employee.salary.revise` (never the employee or whoever recommended it) approves, optionally at a different %, or rejects with a reason; applying creates salary revisions from the effective date on each employee's current structure. Salary amounts appear only with `employee.salary.view` in scope; budget use only with organization-wide salary access. Rules in `hike-rules.ts` (unit-tested). Web: Salary increases page per cycle (from the cycle and from completed reviews).
- **Resume parsing** (migration `20261107100000_candidate_skills`: `candidates.skills`). `POST /recruitment/resumes/parse` reads a PDF or .docx resume and returns name, email, phone, LinkedIn, current title and company, experience (a stated total or the job date ranges, overlaps counted once), notice period, location and skills (a built-in list plus the organization's skill catalog). Text is extracted in a separate, memory-capped Node process with a timeout and no secrets in its environment, so hostile files can't affect the API. The candidate form fills empty fields from a resume and attaches it on save; candidates get an editable skills list (filled from their first resume, never overwritten) that the candidate search covers. Rules in `resume-parser.ts` (unit-tested).
- **Excel and PDF report exports**: `GET /reports/:key/export?format=xlsx|pdf` (CSV stays the default). The workbook has a summary sheet with the headline figures and a sheet per table with typed numbers, percentages, money and dates, a frozen header and filters; the PDF (A4 landscape) has the headline figures and every table with headers repeated across pages and page numbers. Report titles and headline figures moved to the shared contracts (`REPORT_META`) used by both the screens and the exports. The report page's export is a menu (Excel, PDF, CSV).
- Dependencies: `pdf-parse`, `mammoth`, `exceljs`, `pdfkit` (dev: `@types/pdfkit`, `jszip`).
- Tests: backend unit `loan-rules`, `roster-rules`, `skill-rules`, `hike-rules`, `resume-parser`, `report-export` and additions to `exit-rules`, `benefits-rules`; e2e `loans`, `attendance-extended`, `skills`, `hikes`, `resume-parsing` and additions to `exit`, `benefits`, `reports`.

### Fixed
- An empty `S3_ENDPOINT` in `.env` no longer fails environment validation.
- `mail.service.spec.ts` builds a complete production environment (S3 storage settings).
- Integration tests for exit, benefits, loans, compliance, training and reports no longer fail between 18:30 and 24:00 UTC: they take "today" in the organization's time zone (`orgToday` / `orgDays` in `test/helpers.ts`), as the API does, instead of UTC.

## [1.9.0] – 2026-11-06 – Reports

### Added
- Reports API (`/reports`), computed from live data (no new tables). HR reports (`reports.view`) cover the employees within the caller's scope — a department manager sees their department; payroll cost (`reports.payroll.view`) and recruitment (`reports.recruitment.view`) are organization-wide. Every report returns its figures as tables; CSV export (`reports.export`, plus the report's own permission) contains those tables and is audited.
  - Headcount on a day: headcount, serving notice, joiners in the last 30 days, average tenure, share of women; by department, location, employment type, gender and grade; a 12-month trend of month-end headcount, joiners and leavers.
  - Attrition over a period (up to two years): headcount at the start and end, joiners, leavers (last working day passed), resignations versus other exits, attrition over the average headcount and annualized; by department, type of exit, reason and length of service, and the list of leavers.
  - Leave starting in a period: days taken, requests, pending and rejected, people on leave and days per person; by leave type, department and the people with the most days.
  - Payroll cost by month for approved and paid runs (up to 24 months): gross, deductions, net, employer contributions and cost to company; by department for the latest run.
  - Recruitment over a period: applications, candidates, hires and conversion, offers released, accepted and declined, acceptance rate and median days to hire; by current stage, source and job.
- Rules (`reports-rules.ts`, unit-tested): who is employed on a day (including the last working day), leavers in a period, the monthly trend, attrition and annualized attrition, tenure bands by completed years, counts with shares, medians and month ranges.
- Web: HR operations → Insights → Reports (the reports the caller may see) and a report page with period and department filters, summary cards, a monthly chart, tables and CSV export; the HR dashboard shows headcount, people serving notice, joiners and leavers this month.
- Tests: backend `reports-rules.spec.ts` and `reports.e2e-spec.ts` (headcount and attrition with a recent joiner, an exited employee and a recorded termination; department-scoped managers; date validation; CSV export and its permission; tenant isolation; leave, payroll cost from an approved run and recruitment with their permissions); Playwright `reports.spec.ts` (a new employee raises the headcount; export; every report and the dashboard load). The e2e setup also enables Reports; report pages are in the responsive checks.

## [1.8.0] – 2026-11-06 – Exit

### Added
- Migration `20261107030000_exit`: `exit_cases` (one open exit per employee; notice period and its end, requested and confirmed last working day, notice waiver, exit survey, rehire eligibility), `exit_clearance_items` (with dues to recover) and `exit_settlements` (one per exit; computed lines, inputs and totals). Composite tenant foreign keys and check constraints; all models tenant-scoped. An exit keeps the employee from being deleted.
- Rules (`exit-rules.ts`, unit-tested): notice end (resignation day counted), days of notice not served, length of service, gratuity years (a part year of six months or more counts), gratuity eligibility (5 years of continuous service; none on death; 1 year for fixed-term / contract employees) and amount (15/26 × monthly Basic × years, capped at ₹20 lakh), daily rates (Basic ÷ 26, Basic ÷ 30 or gross ÷ 30), the settlement due date (two working days after the last working day, Code on Wages section 17(2)) and the full & final statement.
- Exits: employees resign (`exit.resign`) with a reason and preferred last day, and can withdraw until it is accepted; their manager and HR are told. The manager (`exit.approve` in scope) or HR (`exit.manage`) accepts it with the last working day and an optional notice waiver — never their own. HR records employer-initiated exits (termination, retirement, end of contract, death, other). Accepting starts the notice period: the employee becomes ON_NOTICE with the exit date set to the last working day (so payroll prorates the final month), with a status-change entry in their history and six standard clearance steps. HR can change the last day, cancel the exit (the employee is active again) and complete it on or after the last working day once clearance is done (the employee becomes EXITED).
- Clearance: manager handover, IT, admin, finance and HR steps (more can be added); `exit.clearance.manage` or HR clear them, recording dues to recover; the manager clears the handover.
- Exit survey (employee, once; answers only for `exit.manage`) and rehire eligibility.
- Full & final settlement (`payroll.settlement.manage`, never for one's own exit): leave encashment from the encashable leave balances, gratuity (eligibility can be overridden with a reason), notice pay recovered from a resigning employee or paid in lieu by the employer (unless waived), clearance recoveries and manual lines (bonus, the TDS worked out by payroll…); draft → submitted → approved by someone other than whoever worked it out or submitted it → paid with a reference. Approval posts the encashed leave to the leave ledger and shows the statement to the employee; recoveries and the last day are fixed once submitted. The final month's salary stays with the regular payroll.
- Web: My Workspace → Resignation & exit (resign, notice and last day, clearance progress, exit survey, printable settlement statement); HR operations → Exits (waiting for me / all; exit page with acceptance, last-day changes, completion, cancellation, clearance, exit survey, rehire eligibility and the full & final settlement with its approval and payment).
- `LeaveModule` exports `LeaveBalanceService`.
- Tests: backend `exit-rules.spec.ts` and `exit.e2e-spec.ts` (resignation, notifications, department-scope acceptance, ON_NOTICE with history, clearance permissions and recovery, exit survey privacy, settlement with leave encashment, gratuity, notice recovery, clearance recovery and TDS, segregation of duties, leave ledger, employee statement, payment, completion to EXITED; employer-initiated termination, no salary, cancellation back to ACTIVE, withdrawal, tenant isolation); Playwright `exit.spec.ts` (an employee resigns, HR accepts, clears, completes the exit and sees the no-salary error on the settlement; the employee sees the completed exit). The e2e setup also enables Exit; exit pages are in the responsive checks.

## [1.7.0] – 2026-11-06 – Compliance

### Added

- Policies (`compliance.manage`): category, owner, summary, audience, acknowledgement deadline and review cycle; draft → publish (version 1 always asks for acknowledgement; later versions ask again only for material changes; "what changed" and an effective date shown to employees) → new versions → retire. PDF policy documents (type checked, up to 10 MB), review due reminders on the page and "mark reviewed", acknowledgement tracking (pending first, search, CSV export) and reminders to everyone who hasn't acknowledged (at most once a day; employees are also notified when a version needing acknowledgement is published). Drafts can be discarded; only drafts can be deleted.
- Employees (`compliance.view`, `compliance.policy.acknowledge`): the policies that apply to them with what is still to acknowledge and by when, the current version's text or PDF, and acknowledgement with an explicit "I have read and understood" confirmation.
- Statutory calendar (`compliance.manage`): obligations with an authority, owner, schedule (monthly on a day, or specific dates) and active period; due dates three months at a time plus anything still overdue from the last 12 months; recording a filing with its date, reference and receipt (only for real due dates, never in the future, once; late filings flagged); undoing a filing; obligations with filings are deactivated rather than deleted. Recommended obligations are added on request and can be edited.
- The HR views (policy lists, acknowledgement tracking, calendar and receipts) need `compliance.manage`: `compliance.view` isn't scopable, so it can't tell an employee from HR staff. Permission descriptions updated accordingly. `addMonths` moved to `common/utils/zoned-time`.
- Web: My Workspace → Policies (to acknowledge / all, reading page with acknowledgement); HR operations → Compliance → Policies (list with acknowledgement progress and review dates; policy page with draft editor, PDF, publishing, versions, acknowledgements, reminders and export) and Statutory calendar (due dates by month with filing, obligations).
- Tests: backend `compliance-rules.spec.ts` and `compliance.e2e-spec.ts` (drafts, publishing with notifications, acknowledgement with evidence, tracking and access, reminders once a day, CSV, minor versus material versions, overdue, targeted policy with a PDF, reviews, retirement, deletion, tenant isolation; recommended obligations, schedule validation, owner, overdue due dates, filing rules with a receipt, undo and deactivation); Playwright `compliance.spec.ts` (HR publishes a policy, an employee reads and acknowledges it, HR sees it acknowledged; HR adds the recommended calendar and records a filing). The e2e setup also enables Compliance; compliance pages and the policy page are in the responsive checks.

## [1.5.0] – 2026-11-06 – Grievances and POSH

### Added
- Migration `20261107000000_grievance`: `grievance_cases` (references GRV-0001 / POSH-0001 numbered separately per organization), `grievance_notes`, `grievance_attachments` and `grievance_events`. Composite tenant foreign keys and check constraints; all models tenant-scoped. A complaint keeps the complainant from being deleted; deleting the respondent keeps the case (`ON DELETE SET NULL ("respondent_id")`).
- Confidentiality: complaint details (description, witnesses, POSH delay reason, interim relief, conciliation request), notes and resolutions are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM, `DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY`); audit entries carry only the case reference.
- Access: employees raise cases (`grievance.submit`) and see only shared notes, files and events. Grievances are handled by `grievance.manage` (all) and `grievance.view` (cases assigned to them: read and add notes). POSH complaints are visible only to the complainant and the Internal Committee (`grievance.posh.access`, a restricted permission assigned through a custom role) — never HR or Organization Admin. Nobody can see or handle a case they are the respondent of, handle their own case, or be assigned a case they are involved in.
- Rules (`grievance-rules.ts`, unit-tested): 30-day target for grievances and 90 days for POSH inquiries (section 11(4)); POSH filing within 3 months of the incident, later complaints need a reason for the delay and are flagged to the committee (section 9); one reopening within 15 days of a resolution (POSH findings are appealed to the appellate authority instead, section 18); closing once accepted, after the reopening window or after a reopening; POSH annual report figures (section 21, rule 14).
- Lifecycle: submitted → in review (acknowledged or assigned) → investigating → resolved (outcome; findings shared with the complainant, actions internal) → closed; the complainant can withdraw an open case, accept a resolution or ask to reopen it. POSH settlement only by conciliation at the complainant's request (section 10). Priority, assignment to eligible handlers or committee members, messages and internal notes, evidence files (PDF / images up to 5 MB, type checked from the contents, 20 per case; opening one is audited). Notifications to grievance managers or the committee on new cases, to the assignee, and to the complainant on every step (titles carry only the reference).
- Web: My Workspace → Grievances (raise a grievance or POSH complaint with the committee members named, evidence upload, progress tracker, messages, resolution with accept / reopen, withdraw); HR operations → Employee relations → Grievances (assigned to me / all grievances / POSH complaints with open, overdue and unassigned counts; case page with handling, status actions, resolve dialog, internal notes and timeline) and POSH annual report (printable).
- `usersWithPermission` moved to `employee-people.ts` for reuse.
- Tests: backend `grievance-rules.spec.ts` and `grievance.e2e-spec.ts` (full grievance flow with encryption, internal notes and files hidden from the complainant, handler eligibility, reopening once and closing; POSH complaints hidden from HR and admins, committee handling, late filing, settlement rule, annual report, a committee member named as respondent excluded; withdrawal, acceptance, tenant isolation); Playwright `grievance.spec.ts` (an employee raises a grievance with evidence, HR assigns, adds an internal note, investigates and resolves, the complainant accepts; a POSH complaint can't be found or opened by HR). The e2e setup also enables Grievances; grievance pages, the form and the case page are in the responsive checks.
